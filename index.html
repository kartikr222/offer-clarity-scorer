The Revenue Leak Architecture™

Every deal that doesn't close, and every renewal that doesn't happen, leaves a trace. The Architecture is a five-zone map of your revenue lifecycle — from the moment a prospect first hears about you to the moment they either renew or walk. We diagnose which zone is losing you the most money, and why.

  1. First contact

    The Silent Zone

    Prospects who showed real intent — a demo request, a pricing page visit, a warm referral — and then heard nothing back fast enough to matter. The interest was there. The response wasn't.

  2. Early conversation

    The Trust Gap

    Deals that stall because the buyer isn't convinced you understand their problem, or can't tell your offer apart from three competitors' identical decks. Interest cools into hesitation.

  3. Mid-cycle

    The Follow-Up Graveyard

    Opportunities that were live, warm, even verbally agreed — and simply stopped being followed up with the discipline they needed. Not lost to a competitor. Lost to silence.

  4. Post-sale

    The Handoff Fracture

    The moment a deal moves from sales to delivery, and the context, promises, and expectations don't move with it. The customer notices immediately. Trust takes the hit.

  5. Renewal

    The Renewal Blind Spot

    Accounts that were never at risk on paper — until the renewal conversation surfaced problems nobody had been tracking. No early warning. No time to fix it.
