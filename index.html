The Revenue Leak Architecture™

Every deal that doesn't close, and every renewal that doesn't happen, leaves a trace. The Architecture is a five-zone map of your revenue lifecycle — from the moment a prospect first hears about you to the moment they either renew or walk.

  1. 01 · First contact

    The Silent Zone

    Real intent appears — then the response comes too late to matter. Interest was there. The follow-through wasn't.

  2. 02 · Early conversation

    The Trust Gap

    The buyer isn't convinced you understand the problem, or can't tell your offer apart from familiar alternatives.

  3. 03 · Mid-cycle

    The Follow-Up Graveyard

    Live opportunities cool because disciplined follow-up disappears. Not lost to a competitor. Lost to silence.

  4. 04 · Post-sale

    The Handoff Fracture

    Promises, context and expectations fail to travel from sales into delivery. Trust takes the hit.

  5. 05 · Renewal

    The Renewal Blind Spot

    Problems surface at renewal because nobody was tracking the signals early enough to act.
