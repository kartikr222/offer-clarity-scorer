The Revenue Leak Architecture™

Every deal that doesn't close, and every renewal that doesn't happen, leaves a leak. We've mapped the seven zones where revenue leaks happen — and the root causes in each zone.

  1. 01 · First contact

    The Silent Zone

    Real intent appears — then the response comes too slow, or the reply feels generic. The buyer goes quiet. What happened: no one read the signal.

  2. 02 · Discovery

    The Clarity Zone

    The conversation starts, but your offer never lands clearly. The prospect understands what you sell, but not why it matters to them. What happened: messaging was about you, not them.

  3. 03 · Proposal

    The Price Zone

    They ask "How much?" and the number stops the conversation. Not because the price is wrong, but because the value wasn't proven first. What happened: clarity fell apart before the ask.

  4. 04 · Decision

    The Consensus Zone

    Your champion gets it. But when they take it to the committee, it doesn't travel well. The story breaks down in retelling. What happened: the offer wasn't sticky enough to survive the game of telephone.

  5. 05 · Closing

    The Trust Zone

    Everything's aligned, but something feels off. Legal, security, or procurement raises questions that should have been answered in messaging. What happened: objections were hiding in plain sight.

  6. 06 · Onboarding

    The Handoff Zone

    Deal closes. Then implementation starts, and the customer realizes the offer they bought isn't what they expected. What happened: promises made in sales weren't matched by delivery.

  7. 07 · Renewal

    The Value Zone

    The contract is up. The customer doesn't see enough value to renew, or they never saw the value in the first place. What happened: no one proved ROI in real terms.
