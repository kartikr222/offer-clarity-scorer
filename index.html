    Where the leaks usually are

    Every one of these questions maps to a place prospects quietly drop off — before they ever tell you why.

    1. 1

      The one-sentence test

      If you can't say what you do in one breath, neither can the person who's supposed to refer you.

    2. 2

      The measurable result

      "Helping businesses grow" isn't a promise. A number, a timeframe, or a before/after is.

    3. 3

      The fit filter

      Offers that are for everyone convert like they're for no one. Exclusion builds trust.

    4. 4

      The price clarity

      Confusion about cost is the single fastest way to lose a warm lead at the finish line.

    5. 5

      The next-step clarity

      People don't act on maybes. One clear next step beats five good options.
